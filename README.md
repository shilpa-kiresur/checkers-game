# International Checkers

A browser-based International Checkers game with AI opponent, undo, and win/loss detection.

## How to Play
- Open the game in your browser.
- Click your piece to select, then click a valid square to move.
- Capture all opponent pieces to win.
- Click "Play Again" when the game ends.

## Features
- AI opponent with forced captures
- King piece moves multiple squares
- Undo move button
- Win/Loss detection with Play Again option
- Responsive design

## Rules
- Pieces move diagonally forward only
- Kings move multiple squares diagonally
- Capturing is mandatory
